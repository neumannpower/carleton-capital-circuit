Get involved

Volunteer

Join the event crew at Carleton and help deliver a two-day FIRST robotics exhibition featuring FRC and FTC teams, robots, and match-style demonstrations.

Join the event crew

Volunteer opportunities span field operations, queuing, inspection and technical support, scoring, announcing, load-in, photography, team support, and guest-facing event delivery across the FRC and FTC exhibition areas. Some roles benefit from prior FIRST experience or training, while others are well suited to first-time volunteers.

Who should sign up?

FIRST alumni, university students, faculty and staff, team mentors, parents, industry partners, and community members are all welcome to express interest.
