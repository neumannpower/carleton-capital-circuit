Exhibition format

Two days of FRC and FTC robotics.

Carleton Capital Circuit is designed as an exhibition: teams will demonstrate their robots in live match-style sessions that simulate the formats used in FIRST Robotics Competition and FIRST Tech Challenge. The focus is on showing the robots in action, giving teams meaningful field time, and giving visitors a close look at the engineering, strategy, and teamwork behind both programs.

FRC

Full-size robot exhibition matches

FRC teams will run demonstration matches using the field and match structure to recreate the pace, coordination, and robot interaction of FRC play in an exhibition setting.

FTC

FTC match demonstrations

FTC teams and robots will be part of the two-day program with match-style demonstrations that showcase the scale, control systems, strategy, and student engineering of FIRST Tech Challenge.

Carleton Capital Circuit partners

Host institution

Connecting FIRST with engineering at Carleton.

Hosted with Carleton University’s Faculty of Engineering and Design, Carleton Capital Circuit brings FRC and FTC teams into a university setting where students can demonstrate their engineering, share their robots with the public, and connect FIRST participation with post-secondary engineering, student leadership, and Ottawa’s technology community.
