Team Registration

Register your FRC or FTC team for Carleton Capital Circuit. There is no registration fee for teams. The two-day exhibition features match-style demonstrations for both FIRST Robotics Competition and FIRST Tech Challenge robots.

Register your team

Use the registration form below to provide the team and contact information needed for exhibition scheduling, load-in, team communication, and onsite logistics. The organizing team will follow up with program-specific details for FRC and FTC participants.

Team registration is open, and there is no registration fee. Complete the Google Form below. If the embedded form does not load on your device, use the “Open Google Form” link at the top of the form.
