November 27–28, 2026 • Ravens Nest Gymnasium

Team information

Plan your weekend

Team load-in is scheduled from 9:00 AM Friday, November 27. Saturday pits open at 8:00 AM. See the current team schedule for the full program. All times are Ottawa local time and may change with event operations.

Load-in and parking

The current load-in manual is a draft. Confirmed vehicle routing, unloading, parking and venue access instructions will be included in the next team update and posted here.

Exhibition playoffs

The current FRC schedule plans four alliances with four teams per alliance, roster rotation between matches, and a double-elimination playoff segment beginning at 2:30 PM Saturday. Alliance selection is scheduled for 1:00 PM, followed by lunch from 1:30–2:30 PM. Detailed selection, rotation and playoff rules are being finalized and will be shared with teams before alliance selection. FTC demonstration arrangements will be confirmed separately.

Travel and accommodation

Ottawa Tourism has provided an Ottawa Marriott preferred-rate booking link. Their stated qualifying stay window is November 26–28, 2026. Check the exact eligible check-in and checkout dates, current rate, availability and cancellation conditions before booking, particularly for a Saturday-night stay.

Additional accommodation options are being confirmed. No hotel availability is guaranteed by event registration.

Explore Ottawa

Ottawa Tourism’s Flash Your Badge program offers participating-business discounts for an event badge holder and a guest. Check the current offers and conditions. Event-specific badge instructions will follow.

Second robots, tours and meals

Please confirm second robots directly with organizers so we can finalize pit space and match entries. Campus tour details and available food services will be updated when arrangements are confirmed. Team meals should not be assumed to be included.
